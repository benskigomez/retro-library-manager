Identify and import

For an archive (.zip/.7z) or a folder of files when you don't know which system(s) they belong to — e.g. a mixed download. Each file is identified by its header, its extension, what is inside per-game zips, or its folder name. You confirm anything ambiguous, then get a normal filtered dry run per system. Nothing is written until you run it, originals are left where they are, and existing games are never overwritten.

…or upload from this device

Uploads go to _manager/uploads/ on Jarvis (a folder keeps its sub-folders). For many gigabytes, copying to \\jarvis\roms over SMB and using Browse is faster. Phones can upload a single archive but usually can't pick a folder.
